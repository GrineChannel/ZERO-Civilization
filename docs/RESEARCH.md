# Scientific institutions — option 2

## Implemented
- Abstract schools, universities, academies and institutes.
- Each costs treasury to establish, has recurring upkeep, staffing requirement and research output.
- Education improves from active institutions.
- Five technologies with prerequisites, progress and unlock persistence.
- Commands: `!civ institution school`, `!civ institution university`, `!civ focus engineering`, `!civ research`, `!civ technologies`.
- Research updates during the existing bounded society cycle, without entity spawning.
- Up to 12 institutions per civilization.

## Limitations
- Institutions are abstract records, not Minecraft buildings or physical scholar NPCs.
- Researchers use a bounded local count of adults but are NOT reserved across the economy and military workforce simulations.
- Unlocks are recorded but not yet connected to construction recipes, military power or production multipliers.
- No actual individual scholars, training history, research specializations, exchange programs or knowledge diffusion.
- Not tested in Minecraft Bedrock; use a disposable test world.
